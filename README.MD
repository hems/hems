### Hello 👋

From design to database, from prototype to profit, from raise to folding: in the last twenty-something years I have seen it all.

I have been writing code since 1997 and using Git since 2008. At Hi-ReS! London I led engineering on campaigns for Christopher Nolan's Interstellar, Chanel, and Dolce & Gabbana. I built Feeld's backend from the ground up and watched it scale past two million users. I co-created a crypto social network and raised a round from BitMEX. I wrote Ethfinex's first SDK on top of the 0x protocol, and I have been shipping multi-chain EVM applications since.

In my spare time I created [nowhere.chat](https://www.nowhere.chat), a location-based public chat room, co-founded a record label, and most recently composed a piece for Spatial Festival, performed on 72 speakers in Saal 2 at the legendary Funkhaus Berlin.
